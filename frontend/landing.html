System Context

The Land Administration Challenge in India

Physical records and fragmented registry processes create severe social friction, dispute backlogs, and economic drag across rural and peri-urban India.

66% of Civil Cases

According to NITI Aayog studies, two-thirds of all pending civil cases in Indian courts relate to land and property disputes, averaging 15+ years for final adjudication.

100M+ Brittle Records

Physical Jamabandis, Khasras, and village Tippon maps stored in Tehsil record rooms deteriorate from moisture, handling, torn margins, and ink degradation.

Multilingual Terminology

Revenue deeds span regional vernaculars (Devanagari, Marathi, Telugu) intertwined with archaic Urdu and Persian administrative phrasing.

Credit Bottlenecks

Title ambiguity delays Kisan Credit Cards (KCC) and institutional mortgages, locking billions in agricultural collateral and creating high fraud risks for banks.

Lifecycle

End-to-End Operational Pipeline

Four rigorous stages from raw paper deed ingest to legally binding public digital title.

STAGE 01

Deed Ingestion

Scanning paper documents or uploading deeds. OCR layout engine extracts Khasra #, Khata, owners, and geodetic boundary polygons.

STAGE 02

Spatial Validation

Shapely computational geometry checks boundary boundaries against all neighboring cadastral parcels. Co-sharer ownership sums are verified.

STAGE 03

HITL / Adjudication

If confidence < 85% or an overlap is detected, the parcel routes to the Revenue Officer console for field survey demarcation and decree issuance.

STAGE 04

Certified Title

Verified records are committed to the SHA-256 audit ledger, made available for citizen title searches, and cleared for bank loan collateral.
